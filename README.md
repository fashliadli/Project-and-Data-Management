# Project and Data Management

Projects from my five years at Nutrifood Indonesia (distribution, project and data management), before my M.Sc. in Hydro Science and Engineering. They show how I turn sales and distribution data into decisions: customer segmentation, a single checklist for field teams, and stock monitoring dashboards.

> **A note on confidentiality:** These projects use internal company data. This repository contains no company data, documents, screenshots or internal links. Customer names, sales values and system addresses are left out, and results are described in general terms.

## Projects

| Folder | Project | Year | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`rfm-customer-segmentation-yogyakarta/`](rfm-customer-segmentation-yogyakarta/) | Customer segmentation with RFM analysis | 2020 | Part of an area business review for Yogyakarta: grouping distributor customers by recency, frequency and spend, with a treatment for each group |
| [`integrated-outlet-checklist-clue/`](integrated-outlet-checklist-clue/) | CLUE: one integrated checklist for modern retail outlets | 2023 | Team project that replaces five separate tools for the field team with one checklist in the BI platform |
| [`gimmick-stock-monitoring-gisel/`](gimmick-stock-monitoring-gisel/) | GISEL: monitoring of gimmick (promotional item) stock | 2024 | Dashboards that follow the stock of promotional items at distributors and providers, with an adjustment process and a plan to roll it out nationally |

## Skills shown across the projects

- **Data analysis for decisions:** turning invoice and stock data into segments, indicators and dashboards that sales and finance teams can act on.
- **Process thinking:** mapping the flow of data and documents, finding where figures differ between sources, and defining adjustment rules.
- **Project management:** problem statement, objectives, cost-benefit view, action plan and timeline, and regular updates to stakeholders.
- **Working with many teams:** sales, key account management, sales operations, IT and finance.
